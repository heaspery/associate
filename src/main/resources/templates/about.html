Our story

The association was founded by a handful of friends who wanted to share what they loved with others. What started as small, informal meetings has grown into a community of members and volunteers.

Today we organise events throughout the year, always with the same spirit: simple, friendly and open to all.

Our mission

We bring people together around shared activities and create a space where everyone feels welcome.
